# Camera research and recognition register

Reviewed 2026-11-15. All 90 catalogue IDs are assessed individually. This is an engineering assessment of what one pose camera can observe, not a clinical exercise-validation study. Existing detectors have logic tests; neither old nor new profiles have been physically validated by this implementation run.

## Recognition contract

- **10 existing**: exact previously configured detector, including distinct mini-squat and knee-push-up options. This is the existing game camera category, not certification of technique.
- **60 assisted**: estimates of the specified visible movement, with `evidence: assisted`, `verified: false`, exact skill ID and profile ID. These cannot establish Flash pace or technique stars. A comfortable excursion and complete visible return are required.
- **20 confirmation**: camera preview and instruction remain available; the user explicitly confirms each repetition or actively held second. No pose event is emitted. Invisible muscle tension, scapular position, unsupported circles and compound sequences are not replaced with a generic squat or Bird Dog counter.

The catalogue transport ID is not a recognition contract. Unknown IDs, preview profiles and mismatched aliases fail closed. Existing save IDs are unchanged.

## Technical basis and limits

Google documents 33 predicted landmarks and per-point visibility/presence. None measures abdominal contraction, foot pressure, contact force, scapular displacement or individual vertebrae. Depth and occlusion make many actions ambiguous from one camera. The per-skill decisions below are our engineering inference from that landmark contract and the movement references; **none of the references validates our thresholds**.

Pixel coordinates are aspect-corrected and normalized by visible torso length. Current raw and filtered landmarks, finite values and minimum confidence are required. Cropped required joints, flagged pose spikes, high capture latency and non-monotonic frames are rejected. Calibration requires a stable candidate for 700 ms; changing visible chains or camera scale triggers recalibration.

A repetition uses start → outward movement → observed extent → stable return. Alternation advances only after a complete event. Opposite-limb skills require separate arm and opposite-leg components. Bilateral jacks, arm sweeps and configured frontal movements need both sides. Pause variants require a stable interval away from start. Tempo variants accumulate only visibly outward-moving intervals; waiting at the start/bottom is not slow lowering.

Hold time sums fresh adjacent frames with the configured posture present. Tracking loss, pause, duplicate frames, gaps over 250 ms and leaving the hold reset partial time. Pauses never retract accepted progress. Static muscular effort itself cannot be verified.

## Sources and their scope

These sources describe base movement patterns. Support, pauses, tempo and combinations in the game are separately configured adaptations. The research did not adopt rehabilitation prescriptions, repetition schedules or maximum range targets.

- **mediapipe** — [Google · Pose Landmarker](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker). Landmark and confidence contract; not exercise-validation evidence.
- **nhsStrength** — [NHS · Strength exercises](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-exercises/). Mini squat, chair rise, calf raise, side leg lift, wall press.
- **nhsSitting** — [NHS · Sitting exercises](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/sitting-exercises/). Seated marching, ankle movement and upper-body rotation; related variation where noted.
- **nhsWarmup** — [NHS · Warming up](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/how-to-warm-up-before-exercising/). March, heel dig, knee lift and shoulder roll; related low-impact combinations are game variations.
- **nhsFlex** — [NHS · Strength and flexibility videos](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-and-flex-exercise-plan-how-to-videos/). Wide squat and supported standing press; pause/tempo are game variations.
- **aaos** — [AAOS · Spine conditioning](https://www.orthoinfo.org/bodypart/back/spine-conditioning-program/). Bracing, bridge, bird dog, plank and curl; game is not a rehabilitation programme.
- **squat** — [ACE · Bodyweight squat](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/135/bodyweight-squat/). Base squat mechanics; support, timing and reach variants are separately configured game adaptations.
- **lunge** — [ACE · Forward lunge](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/94/forward-lunge/). Forward lunge; reverse direction and supported variations require additional heuristics.
- **pushup** — [ACE · Push-up](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/41/push-up/). Base arm cycle and trunk alignment. Hand spacing, incline and tempo are not established by elbow angle alone.
- **kneePushup** — [ACE · Bent-knee push-up](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/13/bent-knee-push-up/). Knee-supported arm cycle.
- **birdDog** — [ACE · Bird dog](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/14/bird-dog/). Opposite-limb reach and return; longer holds are game variations.
- **plank** — [ACE · Front plank](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/32/front-plank/). Forearm support and observable body line; high plank uses push-up start.
- **deadbug** — [ACE · Supine dead bug](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/147/supine-dead-bug/). Supine opposite-limb sequence; smaller leg-only variations share this family.
- **bridge** — [ACE · Glute bridge](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/49/glute-bridge/). Hip raise and return; contact force is not visually measured.
- **prone** — [ACE · Prone shoulder stabilisation](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/249/prone-scapular-shoulder-stabilization-series-i-y-t-w-o-formation/). Distinct W/T/Y arm shapes; continuous snow-angel sweep is a game variation.
- **reverseCrunch** — [ACE · Reverse crunch](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/76/reverse-crunch/). Small pelvis curl; distinguish from knee swinging.
- **scapular** — [CUH · Scapular stabilisation](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/scapular-stabilisation-exercises/). Shoulder-girdle movement; scapular position is not a MediaPipe landmark.
- **catCow** — [ACE · Cat–Cow](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/15/cat-cow/). Spinal flexion/extension; spine curvature is not directly observed by the pose model.
- **shoulder** — [East Lancashire Hospitals · Upper limb exercise videos](https://elht.nhs.uk/services/integrated-msk-pain-and-rheumatology-service/patient-information/exercises/upper-limb-exercise-videos). Wall-slide and scapular movement reference; no rehabilitation prescription is adopted.
- **stability** — [CUH · General stability exercises](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/general-stability-exercises1/). Supine, quadruped and side support; related variations marked in the research register.
- **balance** — [NHS · Balance exercises](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/balance-exercises/). Sideways stepping and controlled single-leg support.
- **boxing** — [England Boxing · Coaching handbook](https://www.englandboxing.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/EB_Boxing-Coaching-Handbook-Part-1_09_02_26.pdf). Straight punch coaching reference; game shadowboxing is an unvalidated kinematic adaptation, not boxing technique grading.

The draft parascapular URL was unavailable; the active register uses the accessible CUH scapular page for related context. The original shoulder PDF and NHS Inform page were blocked to the research browser; the active link is the accessible East Lancashire Hospitals video index. England Boxing’s handbook was indexed but too large to open through the text tool; the straight-punch section was discoverable, while exact uppercut technique was not independently inspected. Shadowboxing remains a broad motion estimate, not a coaching claim.

## Corrections from the interrupted draft

- Advanced Bird Dog is reach → inward elbow/knee action → re-reach → return. A single reach or longer pause cannot count it; it uses confirmation.
- Hollow tuck is not tabletop: elevated legs alone do not establish raised shoulders or active low-back position. It uses confirmation.
- Squat with reach is not a squat alone; skater includes a trailing back tap; backward step with reach is not a lateral jack. These use confirmation pending sequence-specific validation.
- Dead Bug, alternating Superman, pause Bird Dog and standing climber require both components rather than a one-limb scalar. Snow angels use arm-angle sweep, not wrist separation (which can return to zero at overhead).
- Side bends and lateral steps use directional measures instead of assigning the next requested side to arbitrary motion.
- Stillness cannot accumulate repetitions, frozen image time cannot accumulate holds, and waiting cannot substitute for observed lowering.
