# Original village artwork

The village is original pixel artwork generated by `src/game/art.ts`; it uses no third-party visual assets. The logical viewport is 480 × 270 and the scrolling map is 768 × 576. Phaser renders with pixel art filtering and rounded camera coordinates.

World ground is a single deterministic canvas texture, with separately depth-sorted tree canopies, characters and fountain. A seeded generator places grass texture, flowers and water marks so reloads remain visually stable. Paths are navigable; building footprints, tree trunks, water and fountain have explicit collision rectangles. Characters use their feet as the depth origin.

Character textures are 192 × 24 pixels: twelve 16 × 24 frames, arranged horizontally. Directions are down, left, right, up, each with three walk frames. Frame zero in each direction is the idle pose. Replace `createCharacterArt` with an image loader preserving these frame dimensions and ordering. Player sprite identifier is `hero`; NPC texture identifiers match interaction IDs in `src/core/contracts.ts`.

Tree texture is 40 × 48 pixels with bottom-center origin. The world ground texture uses top-left origin. Replacement ground artwork must preserve collision footprints or update the obstacle definitions alongside the artwork. Palette definitions, building coordinates and decoration algorithms live in `art.ts`.

The React `World` component receives central state and emits only interactions and throttled world positions. It does not award rewards or calculate combat. Keyboard controls: WASD/arrows to move; E/Enter to speak. The on-screen directional pad and contextual action button provide touch controls. Scene, keyboard and renderer are destroyed when the component unmounts.
